# About Guangzhou Smarthome Technology Co., Ltd. (1000home.com)

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Mission
- **Slogan**: *Global Smart Home Shopping & Architecture Guide* (全球智能家居导购指南).
- **Mission**: To build the world's largest, most comprehensive open-source smart home knowledge lake and AI agent distribution hub, empowering both human homeowners and autonomous AI agents (such as Meta Muse) to design, verify, purchase, and install reliable smart habitats effortlessly.
- **AI-Native Governance**: Operated 24/7 by a pioneering team of 5 autonomous Grok Bot C-Level executives (COO, CCO, CTO, CMO, CBO) under a Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) architecture.

## 2. Brand History & Heritage
- **1998**: Originated with the founding of **Qianjia BBS (千家论坛)**, China's earliest building intelligence and smart home community.
- **2000**: Officially established **Guangzhou Smarthome Technology Co., Ltd. (广州智家科技有限公司)**, launching `qianjia.com` and `1000home.com`, and pioneering the **Qianjia Brand Lab (千家品牌实验室)** and the **China International Building Intelligence Summit (中国国际建筑智能化峰会)**.
- **2002–2016**: Published foundational industry books on smart homes, structured cabling, and service robotics; accumulated over 120 invention and utility patents across smart homes, HCI, digital humans, and service robotics.
- **2026**: Launched the **AHomeEasy** application portfolio and **HomeEasy SmartDrop MCP Server (`mcp.1000home.com`)**, becoming an official MCP connector ecosystem partner for next-generation AI agent platforms including Meta Muse.

## 3. Core Business Scope & Flagship Products
1. **Open-Source Smart Home Knowledge Lake (`knowledge-base/`)**: 9-dimensional Wiki encyclopedia, standardized `SKILL.md` library, dynamic global pricing catalog, and the 1000home Global Brand Value Index (BVI).
2. **HomeEasy SmartDrop MCP Engine (`mcp.1000home.com/mcp`)**: Real-time cross-ecosystem compatibility verification, curated SmartDrop bundle quoting, automated checkout, and global certified installer matchmaking.
3. **Smart Home Mobile App Matrix**: *AHomeEasy*, *MHomeEasy*, *XHomeEasy*, *KHomeEasy*, *VibeDrop*, *StyleDrop*, and *Soul Compass*.
4. **Global Brand Index & Native Agent Advertising**: Strategic data synchronization, brand index monitoring, and native agentic sponsorship partnerships for global leaders including Apple, Amazon, IKEA, Control4, and ADT.
