# IKEA (IKEA Home smart)

## 1. Brand Positioning
IKEA democratizes smart living globally through affordable, design-forward lighting (TRÅDFRI), motorized blinds (FYRTUR/PRAKTLYSING), air quality sensors (VINDRIKTNING/VINDSTYRKA), and the Matter-enabled **DIRIGERA** hub.

## 2. Core Hardware & Ecosystem Anchors
- **Hub**: DIRIGERA Hub (Matter Bridge supporting Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and Home Assistant).
- **Sensors & Controls**: VALLHORN wireless motion sensor, PARASOLL door/window sensor, BADRING water leakage sensor, SOMRIG shortcut button.
- **1000home Synergy**: High-value building blocks for entry-level and renter-friendly `HomeEasy SmartDrop` packages.
