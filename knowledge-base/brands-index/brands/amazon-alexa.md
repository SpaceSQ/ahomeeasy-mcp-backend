# Amazon (Alexa Smart Home)

## 1. Brand Positioning
Amazon Alexa powers the world's largest installed base of voice-first smart home households, combining Echo smart speakers/displays, Ring security, Blink cameras, and Eero Wi-Fi mesh routers with Frustration-Free Setup (FFS) and Matter Multi-Admin.

## 2. Core Hardware & Ecosystem Anchors
- **Hubs & Controllers**: Echo Hub, Echo Show 15/10/8, Echo (4th Gen with built-in Thread & Zigbee), Eero Pro/Max mesh routers.
- **1000home Synergy**: Primary voice ecosystem supported in `check_smarthome_compatibility` and high-conversion fulfillment channel for SmartDrop bundles.
